### Proteus 8 Professional for macOS (Proteus Studio) v1.0.0

Authentic macOS edition of the Proteus VSM & Circuit Simulation Studio.

#### Highlights
- **Native macOS Experience:** Clean Apple design system, dark mode, native system menu bar (`Proteus Studio`, `File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Tools`, `Design`, `Graph`, `Debug`, `Library`, `Template`, `System`, `Window`, `Help`) with keyboard shortcuts.
- **Authentic Windows Toolbar & Tool Icons:** Extracted 1:1 pixel-accurate icon strips from Windows Proteus 8 Professional (`ITB_VIEW32`, `ITB_FILE32`, `ITB_EDIT32`, `TB_MODES32`, `TB_ORNTS32`).
- **Full Zoom & Pan Navigation:** Interactive Pan (`F4`), Zoom In (`F6`), Zoom Out (`F7`), Zoom to Fit (`F8`), Zoom Area, and live percentage readout badge.
- **Schematic & VSM Simulation Engine:** Real-time analog/digital SPICE engine with animated current flow, live probe readouts, and MCU logic emulation.
- **Extensive Component Library:** 1,500+ microcontrollers (AVR/Arduino, PIC, 8051, ARM), discrete logic, sensors, displays, and analog components.
- **680+ Interactive Sample Circuits:** Pre-bundled real-world circuits ready to inspect, simulate, and modify.
- **Drag-and-Drop Installer:** Self-contained `.dmg` package for macOS Apple Silicon & Intel.

#### Installation
1. Download `Proteus-Studio-1.0.0-mac.dmg` below.
2. Double-click the DMG and drag **Proteus Studio** to your **Applications** folder.
3. Open **Proteus Studio** directly from Launchpad, Spotlight, or Applications.
